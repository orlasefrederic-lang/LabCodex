| Proxy application excluded under Android lockdown loses connectivity | **Targeted policy improvement** | `FirewallService.kt:216` reads `isLockdownEnabled`, `:217` retains the proxy app inside VPN while lockdown is enabled, and `:238` suppresses automatic proxy exclusions. New correctness risk worth a targeted test: the local proxy handshake budget was reduced from 20 s to 6 s (`Upstream.kt:265`, `:401`–`:402`, `:458`) and timeout is reported as a frozen/sleeping proxy (`:405`–`:409`). That timer covers the entire SOCKS CONNECT response, not just local greeting/authentication (`net/Socks.kt:144`–`:147`, `:118`–`:124`), or the HTTP CONNECT response. A conforming proxy can answer the greeting immediately and wait for a remote connection before completing CONNECT; a slow remote destination therefore can trigger this diagnostic even though the local proxy is responsive. The source comment claiming every local proxy replies before dialing (`Upstream.kt:397`) is not a valid general assumption. No real-network failure was reproduced here, so this is a test recommendation and diagnostic accuracy issue, not a confirmed outage in the tested v2rayNG scenario.

No new demonstrated exploit in the agreed personal-use threat model was identified in these files. The review does not establish correctness of lock-down transitions, listener attribution across Android versions, remote SOCKS/HTTP servers, or restart capture; those require the coordinating runtime checks.
